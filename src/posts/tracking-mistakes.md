Every print I misread and every trail I lost, laid out honestly so you don't have to make the same ones.

The first mistake is obvious in hindsight: I was looking down the whole time. Good trackers read the whole landscape — disturbed grass, a branch at the wrong angle, birds going quiet — long before they ever find a clean print.

The second mistake took longer to unlearn: assuming a fresh-looking track was recent. Weather, soil type, and time of day all change how long a print holds its shape.

Still learning. Still getting it wrong more often than right. That's most of what this is.
