Two-ton bulls, a beach that smells like nothing else on earth, and the sound they make when they fight — somewhere between a foghorn and something out of a nightmare.

You smell the colony before you see it. Then the beach opens up and there are hundreds of them, barely moving except to occasionally launch themselves at a rival with a speed that seems impossible for something that shape.

Worth the cold, the smell, and the very long lens.
